Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

We believe in transparency, speed, and privacy. Read how we protect you while providing a seamless educational platform.

1. Our Privacy Commitment

Zero Accounts. Zero Data Collection. SOLMATES is built on the philosophy of frictionless learning. We do not require you to sign up, log in, or provide any personal information like names, emails, or phone numbers to use our core platform.

At SOLMATES (accessible from solmates.in), the privacy of our visitors is our utmost priority. Since we do not operate a user-account system, we inherently minimize any data exposure.

Local Storage (Your Device, Your Data)

To ensure you have a highly personalized experience, we use your browser's Local Storage. This means any preferences you set—such as dark mode toggles, AI chat history, bookmarked tools, or game scores—are saved entirely on your own device. We do not transmit or store this data on our servers.

Push Notifications

We may request permission to send you Push Notifications (via OneSignal or similar services) strictly for educational updates, new features, or reminders. You are never forced to accept this, and you can instantly revoke this permission at any time through your browser or device settings.
