No Sound in Video?

If the video is playing but you can't hear anything, your browser has muted this site.

How to fix it:

  1. Tap the 🔒 Lock icon or Tune icon in your browser's top URL bar.
  2. Select Permissions (or Site Settings).
  3. Find Sound and change it to Allow.
  4. Refresh this page.
