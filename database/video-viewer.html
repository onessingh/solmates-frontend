No Sound in Video?

If the video is playing but you can't hear anything, your browser has muted this site.

How to fix it:

  1. Tap the ⋮ Three Dots menu in your browser's top corner.
  2. Tap on Settings.
  3. Scroll down and select Site Settings.
  4. Tap on Sound.
  5. Turn on the switch to Allow sites to play sound.
  6. Refresh this page.
