SOLMATES Platform Guide

A comprehensive overview of all study materials, AI tools, skills, and resources available for DU SOL MBA students.

1. The Homepage

The SOLMATES Homepage serves as the central dashboard for DU SOL MBA students, offering a quick-access portal to all major platforms, tools, and the real-time notification feed.

2. The Database

The core academic engine, providing semester-wise curated content:

  • Live Classes: Access schedules and Microsoft Teams/Zoom joining links for official DU SOL live classes.
  • MBA in Healthcare: Specialized curriculum notes and case studies tailored for MBA Hospital and Healthcare Management students.
  • Recorded Classes: Archival footage of past DU SOL lectures to catch up on missed sessions.
  • Notes: In-depth, unit-wise handwritten and digital notes simplifying complex management theories.
  • PYQs: Previous Year Question Papers to help analyze exam patterns and high-weightage topics.
  • One Shot Notes: Highly condensed revision materials for last-minute exam prep.
  • E-Books: Official study materials and reference textbooks available for digital reading.
  • YouTube Videos: Handpicked educational video lectures perfectly mapped to the DU SOL syllabus.
  • Class Materials: Professor-provided PPTs, PDFs, and supplementary reading materials.
